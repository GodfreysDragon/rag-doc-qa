# Northwind Robotics Contractor Guidelines

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

These guidelines apply to freelancers and agency contractors working for Northwind Robotics. Contractors are not employees, so the Employee Handbook does not apply to them unless a section here says so.

## Statement of work

Every engagement needs a signed statement of work (SOW) before work starts. The SOW sets the scope, the day rate, the end date and the project lead who approves the work. Changes to scope or rate need an amended SOW signed by both sides.

## Invoicing and payment

Contractors invoice monthly, by the fifth working day of the following month, through the supplier portal, Ledgerly. Each invoice lists the days worked and quotes the SOW number. Northwind pays approved invoices within 30 days of receipt.

## Time off

Contractors don't accrue paid vacation and only invoice for days actually worked. Planned absences longer than three working days should be announced to the project lead at least ten working days in advance.

## Equipment and accounts

Contractors either receive a loan laptop or use their own device after installing the device-management profile. Northwind accounts expire automatically on the SOW end date; to extend them, the project lead files a renewal request at least 15 days before that date. The IT Security Policy applies to contractors in full.

## Expenses

Travel is only reimbursed when it is pre-approved in the SOW. Approved trips follow the same hotel caps and per diems as the Travel and Expense Policy, and are claimed on the next invoice, not in Spendly.

## Confidentiality and subcontracting

All contractors sign a non-disclosure agreement before they receive access. Work may not be passed on to a subcontractor without Northwind's written consent.

## Ending an engagement

Either side can end an engagement with 30 days' written notice, unless the SOW says otherwise. Loan equipment must be returned within five working days of the last day.
