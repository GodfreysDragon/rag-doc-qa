# Northwind Robotics Procurement Policy

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

Policy reference: FIN-PRC-2026. This policy covers buying goods and services for Northwind from outside suppliers. Expenses you pay yourself while travelling are covered by the Travel and Expense Policy instead.

## Approval thresholds

- **Up to 500 euros:** your manager approves the purchase.
- **500 to 10,000 euros:** a purchase order approved by the cost-centre owner.
- **Above 10,000 euros:** three competing quotes and approval from Finance.
- **Above 50,000 euros:** additional approval from the CFO.

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay below a threshold is not allowed.

## Purchase orders

Purchase orders are raised in Buyline and get a number in the format PO-123456. Suppliers must quote the PO number on their invoice; invoices without one are returned unpaid. Northwind pays supplier invoices 30 days after receipt.

## New suppliers

A new supplier is set up through the supplier onboarding form, which takes about 10 working days. Any supplier that will store or process Confidential or Restricted data must first pass a security review by the IT security team.

## Software and subscriptions

All software purchases go through IT, including free tools that need a company account or company data. Annual subscriptions are preferred over monthly ones. Licence usage is reviewed every quarter and unused seats are cancelled.

## Gifts from suppliers

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from suppliers worth up to 50 euros. Anything above that must be declared to Finance, and gifts of cash are never allowed.
