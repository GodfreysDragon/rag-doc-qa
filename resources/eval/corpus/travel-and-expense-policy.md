# Northwind Robotics Travel and Expense Policy

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

Policy reference: FIN-TRV-2026. This policy applies to all business travel paid for by Northwind Robotics.

## Booking travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, TripDesk, at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made outside TripDesk are only reimbursed when the fare is lower than the cheapest comparable TripDesk option.

Economy class is the default. Business class is reimbursed only for flights longer than six hours. Trains are preferred over flights for journeys under four hours.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is 180 euros in most cities and 260 euros in London, Paris, Zurich and New York. Stays above the cap need approval from your cost-centre owner before booking.

## Daily allowance

While travelling, you receive a per diem of 45 euros per day for meals, or 60 euros in the high-cost cities listed above. On the days you depart and return, half of the per diem applies. When a meal is provided by a conference or a client, deduct 15 euros for that meal.

## Using your own car

Business trips in a private car are reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed separately with receipts. Fines for traffic or parking violations are never reimbursed.

## Expense claims

Expenses under 75 euros can be submitted without a receipt. Anything above that needs an itemised receipt and must be filed within 30 days of the purchase. Claims are submitted in the Spendly app and approved by your manager; claims above 2,000 euros also need approval from Finance.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## Corporate card

Employees who travel more than six times per year can request a corporate card. The card must not be used for personal purchases. A lost corporate card must be blocked in the Spendly app immediately and reported to Finance within one working day.
