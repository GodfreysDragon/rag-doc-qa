# HX2 Robot Vacuum: Owner's Manual

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

Model numbers covered: HX2-S (standard) and HX2-P (pet edition, with a rubber main brush and a larger 600 ml dust bin). The standard HX2-S has a 450 ml dust bin.

## Charging and battery

Before first use, charge the HX2 on its dock for at least 5 hours. The 5,200 mAh lithium-ion battery gives up to 140 minutes of cleaning in Eco mode and about 90 minutes in Turbo mode. When the battery drops below 15 percent, the vacuum returns to the dock on its own and resumes the job after recharging.

Store the vacuum with the battery at around 50 percent if you won't use it for more than a month. The battery should be replaced after roughly 800 charge cycles; the replacement part number is BAT-5200.

## Maintenance schedule

Empty the dust bin after every run. Clean the main brush once a week and remove tangled hair with the cutting tool stored under the bin. Replace the HEPA filter every three months and the side brushes every six months. Wipe the cliff sensors and charging contacts with a dry cloth every two weeks.

## Error codes

- **E-12: Wheel stuck.** A drive wheel cannot turn. Check for cables or thick rugs wrapped around the wheel and lift the vacuum to see whether the wheel spins freely.
- **E-31: Cliff sensor blocked.** Dust or a dark carpet is confusing the downward-facing sensors. Wipe them; if the error continues on black carpet, enable "Dark floor mode" in the app.
- **E-417: Dock communication failure.** The vacuum can't find the infrared signal from its dock. Make sure the dock has 0.5 metres of clear space on both sides and 1.5 metres in front, and that it isn't in direct sunlight.
- **E-58: Bin missing.** The dust bin is not seated correctly. Push it in until it clicks.

## Mapping and no-go zones

The HX2 builds a floor map during its first two runs. Up to four maps can be saved, for example one per floor of a house. In the app you can draw no-go zones and virtual walls; the vacuum will not cross them, but they are ignored during manual remote control.

## Warranty

The HX2 has a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The battery is covered for one year. The warranty does not cover damage from liquids, since the HX2 is not a wet-cleaning device. To make a claim, contact support with your serial number, which is printed on a sticker under the dust bin.
