# Northwind Robotics Data Retention Policy

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

This policy sets how long Northwind keeps each kind of data and how it is deleted. It builds on the data classification in the IT Security Policy.

## Retention periods

- **Robot telemetry:** kept for 13 months, then deleted automatically.
- **Support tickets:** kept for three years after the ticket is closed.
- **Invoices and accounting records:** kept for ten years, as required by tax law.
- **Job applications:** deleted six months after the hiring decision, unless the candidate agrees to stay in the talent pool.
- **Employee records:** kept for six years after the employee leaves.
- **Access logs:** kept for one year.

## Backups

Backups are encrypted and kept for 35 days. Data deleted from the live systems disappears from the backups when the last backup containing it expires; backups are never restored just to recover deleted personal data.

## Customer deletion requests

Customers can ask for their account and fleet data to be deleted. Requests are confirmed within two working days and completed within 30 days. Invoices are kept for the legal retention period even after an account is deleted.

## Legal hold

When the legal team places a legal hold on a matter, deletion of the affected data is suspended until the hold is lifted, whatever the normal retention period.

## Deleting data safely

Retired laptops and disks are wiped with a certified tool or physically shredded, and a deletion certificate is stored for each device. Paper documents with Confidential data go in the locked shredding bins.
