# Northwind Customer Support Service Levels

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

This document describes the support service levels for Northwind Cloud customers on the Standard, Business and Enterprise plans.

## Support channels

Standard customers get email support. Business customers also get live chat during business hours, Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 CET. Enterprise customers additionally get a phone hotline that is staffed around the clock and a named customer success manager.

## Ticket priorities

- **P1 (urgent):** the customer's fleet is down or robots are unsafe to operate.
- **P2 (high):** a key feature is unavailable and there is no workaround.
- **P3 (normal):** a question or a problem with a workaround.

## First response times

For Enterprise customers, the first response to a P1 ticket comes within 30 minutes, at any time of day. P2 tickets get a first response within 4 business hours and P3 tickets within 1 business day. Business customers get a first response within 2 hours for P1, 8 business hours for P2 and 2 business days for P3. Standard customers are answered within 2 business days for all priorities.

## Escalation

If a ticket has not been resolved within five business days, the customer can ask for an escalation. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a support team lead, who contacts the customer within one business day with a plan.

## Missed response times

When Northwind misses a first-response target on an Enterprise P1 ticket, the customer receives a credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee per missed ticket, up to 20 percent per month. Response-time credits are separate from availability credits and must be requested within 30 days.

## Customer satisfaction

After every resolved ticket the customer receives a one-question survey. The support team's target is an average satisfaction score of at least 4.5 out of 5 each quarter.
