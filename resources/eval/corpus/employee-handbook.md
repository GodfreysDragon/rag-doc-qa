# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can set your own schedule, as long as you work 40 hours per week on average over a month. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendees' agreement.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from both your manager and the People team. Northwind pays a one-time home-office stipend of 600 euros, claimable within your first 90 days.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 27 vacation days per year, credited monthly. Up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year and expire on March 31. Vacation requests longer than 10 consecutive working days need four weeks' notice.

Sick leave is not counted against vacation. From the fourth consecutive sick day you need a doctor's note.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the child's first year.

## Equipment

Every employee receives a laptop on a three-year refresh cycle. Hardware faults are reported through the IT portal using ticket category HW-7. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 24 hours.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 euros for courses, books, and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Conference trips paid from the learning budget also include up to two days of paid working time.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from two peers you choose, and a conversation with your manager. Salary adjustments are only made in the October cycle and take effect on January 1.

## Leaving Northwind

The notice period is one month during probation and three months afterwards. Probation lasts six months. Company equipment must be returned on your last working day; the IT team sends a prepaid shipping box to remote employees.
