# HX2 Auto-Empty Dock: Owner's Manual

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

The Auto-Empty Dock is an optional charging dock for the HX2 robot vacuum. After every cleaning run it sucks the debris out of the vacuum's dust bin into a sealed bag, so you only need to deal with dust every few weeks.

## Compatibility

The dock works with both the HX2-S and the HX2-P. The vacuum needs firmware version 3.2 or later; update it in the app before the first auto-empty. The Auto-Empty Dock replaces the standard dock, so unplug the old one and pair the new dock in the app under Settings > Dock.

## Setting up

Place the dock on a hard, level floor against a wall, with the power cable routed through the channel in the base. Plug it in and leave the vacuum on the dock for one full charge before the first run.

## Dust bags

Each 2.5-litre dust bag holds about 45 days of debris in an average home, or less with pets. Replacement bags have part number BAG-25 and come in packs of three. A bag seals itself when you lift it out, so no dust escapes. Bags cannot be emptied and reused.

## Indicator lights

- **Solid white:** ready, charging the vacuum.
- **Flashing orange:** the bag is full or missing. Fit a new bag and close the lid.
- **Solid red:** the airway is blocked; see below.

## Clearing a blockage

When the light is solid red, unplug the dock, open the airway cover on the base and pull out any clog with the hook tool clipped inside the bag lid. Blockages are most often caused by long hair or a sock picked up by the vacuum.

## Noise and quiet hours

Emptying takes about 10 seconds and is loud, around 78 dB. To avoid it at night, set quiet hours in the app; by default the dock then skips emptying between 22:00 and 07:00 and empties the vacuum after its next run.

## Warranty

The Auto-Empty Dock has a one-year limited warranty, separate from the vacuum's own warranty. Dust bags are consumables and are not covered.
