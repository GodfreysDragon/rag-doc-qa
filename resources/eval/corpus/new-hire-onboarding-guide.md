# Northwind Robotics New Hire Onboarding Guide

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

Welcome to Northwind! This guide covers your first three months.

## Before your first day

Remote hires receive their laptop by courier about a week before their start date. Your accounts are activated at 09:00 on your first day, and your manager sends you a calendar with your first week's sessions.

## Day one

On your first day, set up Vaultline and multi-factor authentication before anything else; you can't reach email from outside the office without them. Then add your bank details in the HR system, Peoplebase. If your bank details are in by the 15th, your first salary is paid at the end of that month; otherwise it comes with the following month's payroll.

## Your buddy

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from another team for the first three months. Your buddy answers the questions you'd rather not ask your manager and introduces you to people across the company.

## Mandatory training

Complete the security awareness course within your first 14 days and the data protection course within your first 30 days. Both are in the learning portal and take under an hour each. Engineers also complete production-access training before they are given production access.

## Check-ins

You meet your manager for structured check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. In your first two weeks you agree on probation goals together; the 90-day check-in reviews progress towards them, halfway through the six-month probation.

## Getting help

For IT problems, use the IT portal. For questions about pay, contracts or leave, write to people@northwind.example. Your buddy is the right person for everything else.
