# M1 Robot Lawn Mower: Owner's Manual

_Fictional sample document used by the DocQA retrieval evaluation._

The M1 is a robotic lawn mower for lawns up to 1,200 square metres. It follows a boundary wire that you lay around the edge of the lawn and returns to its charging station when the battery is low or when rain is detected.

## Installation

Lay the boundary wire at least 35 cm from walls and flower beds and 10 cm from paths that are level with the lawn. The charging station needs 2 metres of straight boundary wire in front of it so the mower can dock reliably. Peg the wire every 75 cm; after a few weeks the grass will grow over it.

## Cutting height and schedule

The cutting height can be set between 20 and 60 mm with the dial under the lid. The default schedule mows every day from 09:00 to 17:00; in the app you can pause mowing on specific days. Mowing in the rain is allowed but leaves a less even cut, and the rain sensor sends the mower home after 30 minutes of rain by default.

## Battery

The M1 uses a 2,500 mAh battery that mows for about 70 minutes per charge. A full recharge takes 60 minutes. Over winter, store the mower indoors, fully charged, at a temperature above 0 °C, and recharge it once every three months.

## Error codes

- **E-12: Blade motor jammed.** A stick or thick grass is stuck in the cutting disc. Switch the mower off, wear gloves, and clear the blade area.
- **E-20: Outside boundary.** The mower has left the area inside the boundary wire. Carry it back onto the lawn and check the wire for breaks.
- **E-44: No loop signal.** The charging station isn't sending a boundary signal. Check that the station has power and that the green light is on.
- **E-58: Lifted.** The mower was lifted while the blades were running. Put it back down and press start.

## Blades and maintenance

The M1 uses three pivoting razor blades on a cutting disc. Turn the blades over or replace them every two months in the mowing season. Clean grass from the underside weekly with a brush; never use a garden hose, because water can damage the electronics.

## Warranty

The M1 has a three-year warranty when registered in the app within 30 days of purchase, and two years otherwise. Blades are wear parts and are not covered.
